Welcome to North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery has been a gathering place for our community since day one. We bake in small batches with care, so every visit feels a little like coming home.

What We Are Known For

People come to us for our sourdough, our warm morning pastries, and our cakes for birthdays and celebrations. We use flour from mills close to home and bake in small batches, so whatever you see on the shelf was made that same morning.
