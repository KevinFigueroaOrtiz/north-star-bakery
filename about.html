About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2015, when Clara Bennett began baking bread in her home kitchen and sharing it with neighbors. People kept asking for more, so she opened a small shop on Maple Street. We are still in the same spot today, baking by hand and getting to know the people who stop by.

Where Our Ingredients Come From

We keep things simple and close to home. Our flour comes from a local mill, and we use butter, eggs, and honey from farms nearby. Fresh ingredients make better bread, and buying local lets us support other small businesses in the area.

Meet the Team

We are a small crew, and we are usually the friendly faces you see behind the counter.

  • Clara Bennett is the owner and head baker. She still pulls the first loaves out of the oven each morning.
  • Sam Ortiz is our pastry baker, in charge of the croissants and morning treats.
  • Maya Cole runs the front counter and helps with pre-orders and questions.
